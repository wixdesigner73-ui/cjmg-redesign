Website services

What is website design?

CJMG Creative provides tailored solutions to create visually stunning and user-friendly websites that your specific business needs. Our team works closely with you ensure your site not only looks great but also functions seamlessly, enhancing your online and driving engagement.

We build responsive, clean, and modern websites that look great on all devices and help customers clearly understand who you are. From layout and graphics to navigation and messaging, we design with purpose—to convert visitors into customers.

Website design facts

Your questions, answered.

Domain name?

As the business owner, you own all of the rights to your domain name and website address. If you do not own a domain, we will work together to select the right domain for your business and walk you through the purchase process and also management and maintenance of your domain.

Edit my own site?

Yes. we design and transfer control of the website over to our clients. We offer management guidance upon completion of the design to make sure there is a basic understanding of website maintenance and design.

What’s included in the website process?

Mobile Responsive. SEO (Website, Google, Social). Map & Menu Structure. Stock Images & Forms. Domain (1 Year Included). Hosting (1 Month Included).
