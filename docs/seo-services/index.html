What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving your website so it appears higher in search engine results like Google. The goal is to attract more organic (non-paid) traffic from people searching for your products or services.

SEO FAQ

Honest answers about SEO.

How long does SEO take to work?

SEO is a long-term strategy. Most businesses begin seeing noticeable improvements within 3–6 months, depending on competition, website history, and how aggressive the strategy is.

Can you guarantee #1 rankings?

No legitimate SEO expert can guarantee specific rankings—Google’s algorithm constantly changes. However, we follow proven strategies that significantly increase your visibility, authority, and traffic.

Do I need SEO if I already have a website?

Yes. A website alone doesn’t mean customers will find you. SEO ensures your site ranks for the keywords your customers are searching for, helping you attract the right audience.
